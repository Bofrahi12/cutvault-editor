CutVault — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026 · Publisher: CineVault

The short version

CutVault is a video editor that runs entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share your personal data or your videos. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Your media stays with you

Videos, images, and audio you open in CutVault are processed locally in your browser/app on your device. They are never uploaded to any server. Project data is stored only in the app's local storage on your own device so you can resume editing; you can erase it at any time by clearing the app's data or using the in-app discard option.

What the app downloads

Some optional features download small AI model files the first time you use them, from public content-delivery networks (jsDelivr, Hugging Face, Google storage): a speech-recognition model for auto captions and a person-segmentation model for background removal and tracking. These are one-way downloads of model files — your media and voice are never sent anywhere; recognition and segmentation run on your device. Like any web request, the hosting CDN may see your IP address as part of delivering the file.

Permissions

The app only accesses files you explicitly pick through the system file picker. Exported videos are saved to your device by you. The Android app does not request access to your contacts, messages, location, camera roll, or any other personal data.

Payments

CutVault is free. There are no in-app purchases and no payment data is ever handled by the app.

Children

CutVault is a general-purpose creative tool. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it collects no data at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
